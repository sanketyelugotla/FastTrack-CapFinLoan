High-Level System Overview
Version 2.0  |  March 2026
Prepared for non-technical stakeholders
1. What Is SmartSure?
SmartSure is a complete, end-to-end insurance management platform built to handle every stage of the insurance journey — from a customer browsing plans online, all the way through purchasing a policy, filing a claim, and receiving a payout decision.
The platform serves two distinct groups of people:
Customers — who can browse insurance options, get instant price quotes, buy policies, and track their claims from any device.
Administrators — who manage the entire operation: reviewing claims, adjusting user accounts, setting discount codes, and generating reports for business decisions.
In Plain Terms
Think of SmartSure as the digital backbone of an insurance company. Everything that would normally require phone calls, paper forms, or visits to a branch office can be done through SmartSure's website — quickly, securely, and with a complete audit trail.

3. What the System Does
SmartSure covers five core functions, each operating independently but connected behind the scenes.
3.1 Identity & Account Management
This covers everything related to user accounts and access control.
Customers can register with their email and password, or log in instantly using their Google account.
A one-time verification code is sent by email when a new account is created, confirming ownership of the email address.
Customers can reset forgotten passwords via an emailed verification code.
Administrators can assign roles, view all users, or remove accounts when needed.
3.2 Insurance Products & Pricing
SmartSure supports multiple types of insurance. Currently the platform is structured to handle two main categories:
Vehicle Insurance — covering cars, motorcycles, and other vehicles. Pricing takes into account the vehicle's age, estimated value, and type of coverage chosen.
Home Insurance — covering residential properties. Pricing considers the property's age, estimated rebuild value, and security features.
Each insurance category has sub-types (e.g. Comprehensive vs. Third-Party for vehicles), and administrators can configure the base pricing for each.
3.3 Quotes & Policy Purchase
Before committing to a purchase, customers can request an instant price quote. SmartSure calculates:
The asset's Insured Declared Value (IDV) — the estimated worth of what is being insured, calculated using standard depreciation rules for vehicles or rebuild cost estimates for homes.
The final premium — the amount the customer pays, based on the IDV, the plan chosen, and any applicable discounts.
Customers can also apply discount codes at checkout. The system validates the code, checks eligibility (some discounts are only available to first-time buyers), and applies the reduction automatically.
Once a customer is happy with the quote, they proceed to purchase. The policy starts in a 'Pending' state and becomes 'Active' once payment is confirmed.
3.4 Claims Management
Filing and tracking a claim is fully self-service for customers:
A customer selects the policy they are claiming against and describes the incident.
Supporting documents (photos, reports, receipts) are uploaded directly through the platform and stored securely in the cloud.
Once ready, the customer formally submits the claim for review.
On the admin side:
Administrators see all submitted claims in a central dashboard.
A claim moves through defined stages: Submitted → Under Review → Approved or Rejected.
The customer receives an automatic email notification at each stage change.
A complete history of every status change is recorded and visible to both the customer and admins.
3.5 Administration & Reporting
Administrators have a dedicated control panel that provides:
A live dashboard showing key metrics: total users, active policies, claim counts, and total revenue.
The ability to generate detailed reports covering policy summaries, claims activity, revenue, and user activity — downloadable as PDF files.
A full audit log: every significant action in the system (who did what, and when) is automatically recorded for accountability.

5. Security & Data Protection
SmartSure has been built with data security as a foundational requirement, not an afterthought.
Security Feature
What It Means in Practice
Password Protection
Passwords are never stored in readable form. They are converted into a one-way encrypted format that cannot be reversed, even by system administrators.
Secure Login Sessions
When a user logs in, they receive a temporary digital pass (token) that expires after a set period. The system never stores their password in a session.
Email Verification
New accounts require email confirmation via a time-limited one-time code, preventing fake registrations.
Role-Based Access
Customers can only see and act on their own data. Admins have broader access, but every admin action is logged with a timestamp.
Secure File Storage
Claim documents are uploaded to an encrypted third-party cloud service. Only a secure reference link is stored in SmartSure’s own database.
OTP Lockout Protection
If someone attempts to guess a one-time verification code more than 5 times, the system locks that attempt automatically.
Google Sign-In
Customers using Google to log in never need a password in SmartSure at all. Their Google identity is verified, and a separate secure session is created.
6. System Components at a Glance
SmartSure is built as four independent but connected modules. Each module handles a specific area of responsibility and communicates with the others automatically in the background. This design means that if one area needs to be updated or scaled, it does not affect the others.
Module
Responsibility
Identity & Accounts
Handles user registration, login, password resets, email verification, and Google sign-in. Manages roles (Customer vs Admin).
Policies & Pricing
Manages all insurance products, pricing rules, discounts, quotes, and the full policy lifecycle from purchase to cancellation.
Claims
Handles the entire claims process: creation, document uploads, status tracking, approvals, rejections, and customer notifications.
Admin & Reporting
Provides administrators with oversight tools: dashboards, audit logs, user management, and report generation.
Background Communication
The four modules automatically notify each other when important events happen. For example, when a policy is activated, the Claims module is immediately informed so it can accept claims against it. When a claim status changes, the Accounts module sends the customer an email. All of this happens invisibly and instantly.

9. Reporting Capabilities
Administrators can generate four types of business reports, each downloadable as a PDF:

What It Shows

10. Summary
SmartSure is a modern, fully self-contained insurance management platform that brings the entire customer journey online — from first visit to settled claim — while giving administrators the tools and visibility they need to run the business effectively.
Key highlights:
Complete self-service experience for customers, reducing reliance on staff for routine transactions.
Structured, trackable claim workflows ensuring consistency and accountability.
Role-based access and a full audit trail protecting both customers and the business.
Automatic email notifications keeping customers informed without manual effort.
Flexible pricing engine supporting discounts, first-time buyer offers, and instant quotes.
Detailed reporting tools supporting business intelligence and compliance needs.
